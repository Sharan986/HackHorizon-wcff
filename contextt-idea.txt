## MedChain — Problem Statement

**Domain:** Health & Wellness

---

### Context
Industrial workers in sectors like steel, mining, and manufacturing are exposed daily to occupational hazards — silica dust, excessive noise, and chemical fumes. Conditions like silicosis, noise-induced hearing loss, and chemical toxicity develop silently over years, with no visible symptoms until irreversible damage has already occurred. Most workers in India have no structured system to track their cumulative exposure or assess their health risk proactively. Medical records are scattered, paper-based, and never connected to the work environment. By the time a diagnosis is made, the window for intervention has long closed.

---

### Challenge
There is no accessible tool that connects a worker's occupational exposure history with their medical record data to predict the onset of chronic occupational diseases before symptoms appear. Existing health checkup systems treat each visit in isolation — they do not accumulate exposure data over time, do not compare lab values against occupational risk thresholds, and provide no personalized guidance to the worker, their doctor, or their employer.

---

### Goal
Develop a digital platform where industrial workers can submit their job profile and upload past medical reports to receive an AI-generated occupational health risk assessment. The system should extract relevant health values from uploaded documents using OCR and NER, combine them with scientifically validated exposure estimates derived from WHO and OSHA job-role standards, and run a machine learning model to predict risk scores for conditions including silicosis, noise-induced hearing loss, and chemical toxicity. The platform should provide plain-language explanations of each score using SHAP-based explainability, deliver personalized intervention recommendations to the worker, surface clinical flags for doctors, and present a plant-zone compliance heatmap for employers — making occupational health intelligence accessible, proactive, and actionable for all three stakeholders.
